How it works

Athlete and parent are both part of it from the first conversation.

  1. Discovery call

    Thirty minutes on Zoom with the athlete and a parent. We learn where the athlete is, where they want to go and which job comes first.

  2. Enroll

    If it's a fit, you enroll online. There's no pressure on the call.

  3. Your plan

    An individualized plan built around the athlete's season, delivered through the Trainerize app.

  4. Train, measure, adjust

    The athlete does the work. We measure it, explain the why and adjust as they develop.
