What we believe

You don't need more talent. You need less noise.

Above all

Identity first.

Who the athlete is comes before anything they throw. Their worth is settled by God, not the radar gun or the box score. They compete from that identity, never for it.

  • Childlikeness must be restored.

    Pressure takes the joy out of the game. We bring it back, because athletes compete best when they play like a kid again.

  • Programming is individual.

    Every athlete gets a plan built for their body, their needs and their season. No templates.

  • Progress is not linear.

    Development has jumps, plateaus and setbacks. We measure over seasons, not single outings, and stay patient with the process.

  • Mind, body and spirit.

    Each one has to be addressed to reach peak performance. We train all three on purpose.

Questions parents ask

Who is 323 for?

High school pitchers and their parents. The athlete does the work. The parent is a partner in the plan from the first call.

Is everything remote?

Yes. The discovery call is on Zoom, and programming is delivered through the Trainerize app, so your athlete can train wherever you live.

Do we have to sign up for both programs?

No. You can start with Full Pitching Programming, Mental Game Mentorship or both. Pick the one you're most interested in when you book, and we'll talk through the right starting point on the call.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the program. We walk through it on the discovery call once we understand your athlete. The call itself is free.

Will my athlete throw harder?

We don't promise velocity numbers, roster spots or scholarships. Progress is not linear. What we do commit to is an individualized plan, honest measurement and the reasoning behind every adjustment.

What happens on the discovery call?

Thirty minutes with the athlete and a parent. We learn where the athlete is, where they want to go and what's getting in the way. If it's a fit, we'll recommend a program. If it isn't, we'll tell you.
