Complete Pitching Program

For the pitcher who wants everything. One coach, one plan, every part of the athlete.

Most high school pitchers are training in pieces. They have a throwing routine from one place, a lift from another, and no plan at all for what happens between the ears. Those pieces don't talk to each other. That's how arms get overworked and progress stalls.

The Complete Pitching Program puts it all under one plan. Your throwing, strength, mobility, and mental game are programmed together and adjusted every week around your team schedule. Nothing gets stacked. Nothing gets missed.

We build velocity and command the way they last, through better movement and not more stress. And we make sure you can carry that work onto the mound when it counts.

What's included

  • Individualized throwing program. Built around your team schedule, your workload, and your goals. Constraint-based drills develop velocity and command by improving how you move, not by forcing mechanics.
  • Strength, mobility, and movement prep. Programmed by phase, with a video demo for every exercise. Train like an athlete, not just a thrower.
  • Mechanical analysis and throwing assessment. Bullpen video is reviewed every training block, with clear fixes and the reasons behind them.
  • The full 323 mental game curriculum. Focus, pressure, confidence, routines, and identity, built into your weekly work.
  • Weekly 1-on-1 call. Review the week, adjust the plan, and talk through what's going on in your head.
  • Video feedback within 24 hours. Send video and check-ins through the app. Hear back within a day.
  • Parent updates. A short, honest summary so you always know what we worked on, what improved, and what's next.
  • Access to the 323 Community.

Who it's for

High school pitchers who are serious about getting better and want a coach who sees the whole picture. It's the right fit if your athlete needs a throwing and lifting plan, not just mentorship.

Why parents choose it

You'll know the plan. You'll know the reason behind it. And you'll know your athlete's workload is being managed by someone watching it every week. We respect the team schedule. We adjust instead of piling on.

Commitment

6-month minimum. Five founding spots are open across both programs, and founding athletes keep their founding rate as long as they stay enrolled.

Mental Game Mentorship

For the pitcher who already has a throwing program but loses it between the ears.

Every coach has seen it. The pitcher who dominates the bullpen and unravels in the third inning. The kid who can't shake one bad outing. The athlete who's playing to impress the scout in the stands instead of competing against the hitter in the box.

That isn't a talent problem. It's interference.

Performance = Potential − Interference

Fear of failure, comparison, overthinking, and playing for approval all take away from what your athlete can do. This program removes them.

Mental skills are trained like physical ones. They take reps, structure, and a coach. We give your athlete all three, starting with who they are. Confidence that depends on the radar gun or the box score breaks easily. Confidence rooted in identity holds. We compete from identity, never for it, and we talk about faith openly because that's where it starts.

What's included

  • The 323 mental game curriculum. It starts with a 4-week foundation phase covering identity, focus, pressure, and routines, then builds from there.
  • Weekly 1-on-1 call. We work through what's actually happening on the mound and off it.
  • Message access through the app. Reach out before a start, after a rough outing, or when something is stuck in your head.
  • Pre-game and recovery routines built for you. You'll have a plan for the bullpen, the dugout, and the drive home.
  • Faith-integrated mentorship. Competing freely starts with knowing who you are. We talk about that openly.
  • Parent updates and access to the 323 Community.

Who it's for

Pitchers who already have a throwing and strength plan they trust but aren't performing up to it in games. It's also a strong fit for athletes who carry pressure, comparison, or a bad outing longer than they should.

Why parents choose it

Your athlete gets a mentor who understands the game from the mound and points them back to what matters. You get a clear, structured process instead of "just relax out there."

Commitment

3-month minimum. Five founding spots are open across both programs, and founding athletes keep their founding rate as long as they stay enrolled.

Questions parents ask

Who is 323 for?

High school pitchers and their parents. The athlete does the work. The parent is a partner in the plan from the first call.

Which program is right for my athlete?

If your athlete needs a throwing and lifting plan, the Complete Pitching Program. If they already have a throwing and strength plan they trust but aren't performing up to it in games, Mental Game Mentorship. Not sure? That's what the discovery call is for.

Is everything remote?

Yes. Your program, video feedback and check-ins run through the app, and calls happen online, so your athlete can train wherever you live.

What does it cost?

Prices aren't listed on the site. Program details and founding rates are covered on the discovery call, and the call is free.

Why 6 months for the Complete Pitching Program?

Velocity and command don't change in four weeks. Real movement change takes a full training cycle. Six months gives us time to build, test, and prove it.

Why 3 months for Mental Game Mentorship?

Mental skills are trained like physical ones. Three months gives your athlete enough time to build the habits and use them in real games.

Will my athlete throw harder?

We don't promise velocity numbers, roster spots or scholarships. Progress is not linear. What we do commit to is an individualized plan, honest measurement and the reasoning behind every adjustment.

What happens after I apply?

You'll pick a time for a free 20 to 30 minute call with your athlete and a parent. We'll give you an honest recommendation, including if neither program fits.
