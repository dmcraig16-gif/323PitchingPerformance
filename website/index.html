Why 323 exists

Most young pitchers get a program. A PDF. A velo plan. An app. Then they're on their own.

Nobody watches the video. Nobody asks how school is going. Nobody notices when the confidence goes.

323 exists to close that gap. Every athlete gets a coach who invests in the whole person. The arm, the body, the mind and the faith underneath it.

The name

323 comes from Colossians 3:23. That verse is the standard for every rep and every athlete. We work for the Lord, not the radar gun.

“Freedom is the real swag.”

How it works

  1. Apply

    Five minutes. Tell us where you are and where you want to go.

  2. Intro call

    Athlete, parents and coach. We make sure it's a fit both ways.

  3. Evaluation

    You send video. We break down your delivery, your movement and how you compete.

  4. Your plan

    One plan for throwing, movement and mental game, built around your season.

  5. Every week

    A call, feedback on your work and a text after every outing. Parents stay in the loop.
