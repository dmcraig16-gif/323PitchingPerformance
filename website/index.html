FAQ

Common questions

How does remote training actually work?

You get a login to the athlete app with your full program. You film bullpens and outings on your phone and upload them for review, send data files if you have them, and we meet on scheduled check-in calls. You can message your coach between calls.

Do I need a Trackman or Rapsodo?

No. Phone video is enough to start. If you throw in front of Trackman, or your outings show up on Baseball Savant, send the file and we'll break down your movement, spin and release by pitch type.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the program and how often we meet. We'll go through options on your call, after we know what you need. There's no cost to apply.

Do I have to be a Christian to train here?

No. Every athlete is welcome. Faith shapes how we coach and how we treat people, and devotionals are part of the app, but no athlete is ever required to share our beliefs.

My athlete is under 18. Can I apply for them?

Yes. A parent or guardian can fill out the application. We include parents in the intro call and keep them in the loop on the plan.

How fast will I see results?

Most athletes notice better command and a calmer approach within the first few weeks. Velocity gains depend on age, training history and consistency. We'll set realistic targets on your call.
