Complete Pitching Program

One coach, one plan, every part of the athlete.

Most high school pitchers train in pieces: a throwing routine from one place, a lift from another, and nothing for the mental game. That's how arms get overworked and progress stalls. This program puts it all under one plan, adjusted every week around your team schedule.

What's included

  • Individualized throwing program built around your team schedule, workload, and goals.
  • Strength, mobility, and movement prep by phase, with a video demo for every exercise.
  • Mechanical analysis. Bullpen video reviewed every training block, with clear fixes.
  • The full 323 mental game curriculum built into your weekly work.
  • Weekly 1-on-1 call to review the week and adjust the plan.
  • Video feedback within 24 hours.
  • Parent updates on what we worked on, what improved, and what's next.
  • Access to the 323 Community.

Who it's for

High school pitchers who are serious about getting better and need a throwing and lifting plan, not just mentorship.

Commitment

6-month minimum. Real movement change takes a full training cycle. Founding athletes keep their founding rate as long as they stay enrolled.

Mental Game Mentorship

For the pitcher who has the stuff but loses it between the ears.

Great in the bullpen, gone by the third inning. That isn't a talent problem. It's interference: fear of failure, comparison, overthinking, and playing for approval.

Performance = Potential − Interference

We train mental skills like physical ones, with reps, structure, and a coach. It starts with identity, and we talk about faith openly, because confidence rooted in who you are holds when the radar gun doesn't.

What's included

  • The 323 mental game curriculum, starting with a 4-week foundation in identity, focus, pressure, and routines.
  • Weekly 1-on-1 call on what's happening on the mound and off it.
  • Message access before a start or after a rough outing, with a parent included.
  • Pre-game and recovery routines built for you.
  • Faith-integrated mentorship, talked about openly.
  • Parent updates and access to the 323 Community.

Who it's for

Pitchers who have a throwing and strength plan they trust but don't perform up to it in games, or who carry pressure and bad outings longer than they should.

Commitment

3-month minimum. Enough time to build the habits and use them in real games. Founding athletes keep their founding rate as long as they stay enrolled.

Questions parents ask

What happens after I apply?

Right after you submit, you'll pick a time for a free 20 to 30 minute call with your athlete and a parent. We'll talk through goals and schedule and give you an honest recommendation, including if neither program fits.

Who is 323 for?

High school pitchers and their parents. The athlete does the work. The parent is a partner in the plan from the first call.

Which program is right for my athlete?

If your athlete needs a throwing and lifting plan, the Complete Pitching Program. If they already have a throwing and strength plan they trust but aren't performing up to it in games, Mental Game Mentorship. Not sure? That's what the discovery call is for.

Is everything remote?

Yes. Your program, video feedback and check-ins are all online, and calls happen over Zoom, so your athlete can train wherever you live.

How do you keep my athlete's arm healthy?

Every throwing program is built around your athlete's team schedule and total workload, and we adjust instead of stacking extra throwing on top of games and practice. If your athlete ever reports elbow or shoulder pain, throwing stops, we contact you, and we refer you to a medical provider. We never diagnose injuries.

How does communication with my athlete work?

A parent is included in all communication between coach and athlete. You're on the first call, you get regular updates, and you always know what the plan is and why.

What does it cost?

Prices aren't listed on the site. Program details and founding rates are covered on the discovery call, and the call is free.

Why 6 months for the Complete Pitching Program?

Velocity and command don't change in four weeks. Real movement change takes a full training cycle. Six months gives us time to build, test, and prove it.

Why 3 months for Mental Game Mentorship?

Mental skills are trained like physical ones. Three months gives your athlete enough time to build the habits and use them in real games.

Will my athlete throw harder?

We don't promise velocity numbers, roster spots or scholarships. Progress is not linear. What we do commit to is an individualized plan, honest measurement and the reasoning behind every adjustment.

What we believe

You don't need more talent. You need less noise.

Above all

Identity first.

Who the athlete is comes before anything they throw. Their worth is settled by God, not the radar gun or the box score. They compete from that identity, never for it.

  • Childlikeness must be restored.

    Pressure takes the joy out of the game. We bring it back, because athletes compete best when they play like a kid again.

  • Programming is individual.

    Every athlete gets a plan built for their body, their needs and their season. No templates.

  • Progress is not linear.

    Development has jumps, plateaus and setbacks. We measure over seasons, not single outings, and stay patient with the process.

  • Mind, body and spirit.

    Each one has to be addressed to reach peak performance. We train all three on purpose.
