## Foundation

**For the Lord, not for man.** Colossians 3:23: "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men." Every page answers to that verse.

**What 323 is:** A remote, individualized, faith-based development program for high school pitchers and their parents.

**Why we exist:** We develop every part of the athlete. We raise their ceiling as a pitcher, then make sure they can play up to it.

**Two jobs, always together:**
1. **Raise the ceiling.** An individualized program for higher velocity, a deeper arsenal, sharper pitchability, and more efficient mechanics.
2. **Play up to it.** Mentorship through the mental game and identity, so the athlete performs to the potential they've built.

**Four development areas:**

| Area | Job | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Skill Acquisition | Raise the ceiling | Velocity, arsenal, pitchability, command, mechanics, workload |
| The Engine | Raise the ceiling | Strength, mobility, movement quality, recovery |
| The Mind | Play up to it | Focus, pressure, confidence, routines |
| The Spirit | Play up to it | Identity, character, faith, how you compete and carry yourself |

**Coaching philosophy:** Movement efficiency and athleticism first. Velocity is built through constraints and variability, not forced mechanics. Development is individualized, measured, and built around each athlete's season.

## Visual Summary

- **Feel:** Dark, editorial, clean. Confident and quiet. Never flashy.
- **Base:** True black backgrounds with white type.
- **Accent:** 323 Blue on one key word per headline, links, and the main button.
- **Red:** Sparing. One word, one stat, or one badge per screen. Never body text. Never a background.
- **Scripture:** Always italic serif, set apart in its own block with a blue rule on the left. Never buried in body copy.
- **Headlines:** Tall condensed caps. Short. A statement, not a sentence.
- **Layout:** Generous space, sharp corners (4px radius), strong contrast. One clear call to action per section.
- **Photos:** Real athletes and real work. Bullpens, training, film. No stock hype imagery.
- **Rule:** Every section ends at the application. The discovery call is booked after the application is submitted.

## Brand Voice

**Professional, clear, and grounded in faith.** We speak like a trusted coach: knowledgeable, calm, and direct. The athlete and the parent should both walk away feeling informed and respected.

**Do:**
- Teach. Explain the why behind every drill, plan, and principle.
- Speak to the athlete as a developing competitor and to the parent as a partner.
- State faith openly and with conviction. Never soften it for the audience.
- Back claims with evidence, data, and sound coaching principles.
- Keep sentences short and clear. Vary rhythm so it reads human.

**Don't:**
- No em dashes. No arrow bullets.
- No hype, no guru talk, no "unlock your potential."
- No guaranteed results. No velocity, roster spot, or scholarship promises.
- Nothing that sounds like a robot wrote it.
- No David personal-story content on the 323 site unless he adds it on purpose. That lives on @dc.throws.

**Sounds like:** "You don't need more talent. You need less noise."
**Doesn't sound like:** "Unlock elite velocity with our proven system!"

## Brand Filter

Every page runs through two lines. If it doesn't fit, it doesn't ship.

- **Freedom is the real swag.** Confidence comes from knowing who you are, not from how you look or what the radar says.
- **Less interference, more capacity.** Performance = Potential minus Interference. We remove what's in the way.

## What We Believe

- **Identity first. The biggest one.** Who the athlete is comes before anything they throw. They compete from identity, never for it.
- Childlikeness must be restored. Athletes compete best when they play like a kid again.
- Programming is individual. Every athlete gets a plan built for their body, needs and season.
- Progress is not linear. Measure over seasons, not single outings.
- Mind, body and spirit must each be addressed to reach peak performance.

Pitching Development. Train Like an Athlete. The Mental Game. Identity on the Mound. Pursuit of Excellence.
