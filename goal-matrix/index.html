How to use this: The center box is your ultimate goal. The 8 boxes around it are the key focus areas you'll need to work on to reach it — things like a physical skill, a mental habit, or a relationship. Each of those areas then becomes the center of its own mini-grid, surrounded by 8 specific, actionable steps. Type a focus area once and it automatically fills in both places. Your work saves automatically in this browser — when you're done, click Download PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to create a file you can send to your coach.
