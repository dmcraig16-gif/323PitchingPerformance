Goal Matrix
Colossians 3:23
How to use this: One goal. Eight supporting pillars. Eight specific actions or behaviors for each. The center box is your one goal. The 8 boxes around it are the eight supporting pillars you'll need to strengthen to reach it — things like a physical skill, a mental habit, or a relationship. Each pillar then becomes the center of its own mini-grid, surrounded by eight specific actions or behaviors that bring it to life. Type a pillar once and it automatically fills in both places. Your work saves automatically in this browser — when you're done, click Download PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to create a file you can send to your coach.
What are Export / Import for?

This page saves your work automatically, but only in this browser on this device. Click Export Data to download everything you've typed as a small file you can keep, email, or hand to a coach. Click Import Data to load one of those files back in — useful if you switch devices, clear your browser, or want to pick up where someone else left off.
