How to use this: The center box is your ultimate goal. The 8 gray boxes around it are the key areas you'll need to improve to reach it (e.g. body, control, velocity, mindset). Each of those 8 areas then becomes the center of its own mini-grid, surrounded by 8 specific, actionable steps you can actually work on. Type an area's name once and it will automatically fill in both places. Your work is saved automatically in this browser — when you're done, click Download PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to create a file you can send to your coach.
